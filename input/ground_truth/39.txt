  'UnderFitting' refers to the exact opposite phenomenon - when the model is not able to fit the training data too well and is unable to capture its intricious . Underfitting is a result of  high bias and low variance. Unlike overfitting where the model becomes very complex is underfitting the model is simple (eg. Straight live).Both underfitting and Overfitting are disadvantage and we must try to like a balance so as to reduce both and achieve best prediction results.
