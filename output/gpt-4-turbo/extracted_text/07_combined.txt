10) ROC & AUC curves are the crucial parameters for determination of a training model.
ROC is inversely proportional to AUC, that is,
\[ ROC \times \frac{1}{AUC} \]
AUC is the area under curve i.e., the graph plotted by ROC & the area which is covered by the ROC is its AUC.
These are used for showcasing the performance of a model.

1. Diagram labeled "perfect classifier" with a curve approaching the top-left corner.
2. Diagram labeled "practical classifier" with a curve approaching the top-left corner but slightly towards the center compared to the perfect classifier.
3. Diagram labeled "random classifier" showing a diagonal line from the bottom-left to the top-right corner, indicating a performance similar to random guessing.

Each graph/diagram has axes ranging from 0 to 1, normally representing true and false positive rates in an ROC curve context.
