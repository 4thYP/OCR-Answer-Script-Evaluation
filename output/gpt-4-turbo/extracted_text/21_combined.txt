9) Confusion matrix is a metrics in machine learning through which one can get an idea how to our model has classified or predicted in contrast to the actual result.
   - Confusion Matrix structure:
       - **Predicted value**: TP (True Positive), FP (False Positive)
       - **Actual value**: FN (False Negative), TN (True Negative) 
The main utility of confusion matrix is to find out the accuracy and trade of misclassification our model goes through dataset.

  - TN (True Negative) = 92
  - FP (False Positive) = 3
  - FN (False Negative) = 5
  - TP (True Positive) = 10

    |   |  0 |  1 |
    |---|----|----|
    | 0 | 92 |  3 |
    | 1 |  5 | 10 |

  - **Precision**: Precision = TP / (TP + FP) = 10 / (10 + 3) = 10 / 13
  - **Recall**: Recall = TP / (TP + FN) = 10 / (10 + 5) = 10 / 15
  - **False Negative Rate**: FNR = FN / (FN + TP) = 5 / (5 + 10) = 5 / 15 = 1 / 3
  - **False Positive Rate**: FPR = FP / (FP + TN) = 3 / (3 + 92) = 3 / 95
