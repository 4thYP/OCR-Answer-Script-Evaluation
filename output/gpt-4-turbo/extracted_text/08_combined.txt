9. Confusion Matrix - Confusion Matrix is a comprehensive model evaluation performance measure. This matrix which is visualized as a tabular form and counts the number of actual outputs versus predicted output:
    - **[Table]**
      \[
      \begin{array}{c|c|c}
      & \text{Predicted} & \text{Predicted} \\
      \text{Actual} & \text{Positive} & \text{Negative} \\
      \hline
      \text{Positive} & TP (True Positive) & FN (False Negative) \\
      \text{Negative} & FP (False Positive) & TN (True Negative) \\
      \end{array}
      \]

      - TP - True Positive is model predicted positive class correctly.
      - FP - False Positive is model predicted positive but actual was negative (Type I error).
      - FN - False Negative is model predicted negative but actual was positive (Type II error).
      - TN - True Negative is model predicted negative classes correctly.

      Additional definitions:
      - **TP+FP** = Total true predictions
      - **FN+TN** = Total no predictions
      - **TP+FN** = Total actual positives
      - **FP+TN** = Total actual negatives
